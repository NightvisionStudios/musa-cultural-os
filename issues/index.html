The body over the single. British R&B has never had a deeper bench of writers and producers, and for most of the last decade the arrangement was that they stayed behind the glass while somebody else fronted the record. That arrangement is being renegotiated in public. Barbara Boko and Edith Nelson wrote and produced the bulk of Falling or Flying and produced on Jim Legxacy's black british music (2025); their own debut landed on July 24 with almost no campaign attached. Nia Smith answered a 2024 debut EP with a second one on May 29 and collected a MOBO Best Newcomer nomination for it. Against that, Tamera has thirteen and a half million plays on a single from 2021 and a debut album that has been arriving 'later this year' for three years running, and Finn Askew has cosigns from SZA, Kehlani and Bieber attached to a seventeen-minute tape. Legxacy is the exception that proves the rule: one new single in the twelve months since BBM, and a festival summer running entirely on a record that came out last July. The room will hold a position on a catalog. It will not hold one on a promise.

HOW TO READ A SCORE
MUSA SCORE (1–10) is the taste read — where a name sits against the canon. HEAT (0–100) is how loud the room is right now. The gap between them is the whole point: high score, low heat is a FIND — taste ahead of the room. High heat, low score is NOISE. When both run high and the room is right, that's earned.
